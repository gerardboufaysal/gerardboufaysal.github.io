AI transcription and translation of field recordings in three languages

COAR Global research teams, 2026. In production.

Field recordings in Arabic, Haitian Creole and Burmese transcribed, translated and summarised by two pipelines and filed where analysts work. The review strategy was written before launch: review started at 100 percent against the manual result and stepped down as measured accuracy held. A full translation position is no longer needed.

Problem

Every interview and focus group had to be transcribed and translated before analysis could start. Translation was a bottleneck and a standing position. Recordings arrive in Arabic, Haitian Creole and Burmese, from two collection platforms.

Two pipelines, one job

Both run on the self-hosted automation platform. Convergence of the two pipelines is planned; it has not been built.

A daily schedule pulls each new focus-group submission and its audio attachment from the field data platform. One model analyses and transcribes the audio, then translates it. A ledger in object storage, keyed on the submission identifier, records what has been processed, so a rerun after a failure does not duplicate.

A webhook from the collection platform starts the run. Transcription and translation use a second vendor's model. The run also extracts metadata and generates a summary with key notes. The transcript and the summary are written to the team's shared drive and the analyst is notified in Slack.

Who does what

Review strategy as written

The strategy was written before launch.

  • What is reviewed. Every output in the first month. Now 30 percent of the full sample. Next, a monthly review of selected items.
  • Against what. The manual result of the same task.
  • Who signs. An analyst approves the cleaned transcript. Nothing is indexed in the knowledge platform without that approval.
  • When the share moves. Only as measured accuracy holds. So far, from 100 percent to 30 percent.

The review ladder

The general method

The typical steps are 100, 70, 40 and 5; this workflow stands at 30. The person leaves the routine item; the measurement stays.

Result

A full translation position is no longer needed. Analysts receive the transcript, the translation and a summary where they already work, and the review share is a decision taken on measured accuracy rather than on trust. The share stands at 30 percent of the full sample and is moving to a monthly review of selected items. Nothing is indexed in the knowledge platform without an analyst's approval of the cleaned transcript.

What was learned

  • Write the strategy before launch. What is reviewed, against what, who signs and when the share moves were written down before the workflow went live.
  • A ledger makes reruns safe. The ledger records what has been processed, so a rerun after a failure does not produce a second transcript of the same recording.
  • The share moves on measurement, not on comfort. Review went from 100 percent to 30 percent only as measured accuracy held.

Role

Gerard Bou Faical, Head of Technology and AI Consultant, COAR Global and member of the senior management team, designed and built both pipelines, operates the automation platform, wrote the review strategy and decides the review share on the measured results.

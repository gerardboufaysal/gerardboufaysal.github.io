06 · Independent product, built solo

AI support agent for Odoo and Shopify clients

Designed, built and operated solo. In production since July 2026 for a first client.

A support agent that reads the client's own addon repository or live configuration through read-only tools, answers how-to questions directly and hands bugs and change requests to an engineer as a diagnosis with affected files, a proposed patch and an estimate. Stages, coverage and limits are rules in code; the customer approves every proposal.

At a glance

Users
Small businesses on a support contract for an Odoo ERP or a Shopify store, and the engineer who executes approved work.
Problem
Most tickets are how-do-I questions and small changes whose answer sits in the client's own code and configuration; an engineer had to read it before replying.
Hypothesis
A model with read access to the actual code can carry triage, diagnosis and scoping, because the facts are in the repository, not in a knowledge base someone has to write.
What shipped
Email and portal intake, a tool-use agent with read-only tools on client systems, five ticket types, a staged ticket flow, package and quotation records in the back office, a customer approval step, an engineer hand-off, a hardening pass in August 2026.
Decision owner
The customer approves every proposal; the engineer executes and can correct the diagnosis; an administrator applies hours at resolution.
Success measure
Engineer-free resolution rate, first-response time, clarification rate, proposal acceptance, estimate accuracy, engineer rework, SLA attainment.

A ticket loops between triage and awaiting customer until there is enough information. On the customer's approval it moves to a stage owned by the engineer. The agent never moves a ticket into an engineer-owned stage and never posts in one.

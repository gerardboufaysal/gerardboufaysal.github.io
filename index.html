How each one is run

Review starts at 100 percent and steps down.Full review against the manual result at launch, then down to a sampled check.
Model for judgement, code for rules.Gates, thresholds, scoring and schedules live in code; the model categorises, summarises and explains.
Measured, not assumed.Evaluation sets, review shares set on evidence, retrieval quality measured before and after changes.
Outputs where people work.The applicant record, the inbox, Slack, the shared drive. Adoption is designed in.

Context

COAR Global is an international research organisation of about 180 staff, more than 300 people with its field data collectors, that carries out research for United Nations agencies, with teams in Syria, Haiti, Myanmar and the Middle East and North Africa. Gerard Bou Faical has been its Head of Technology and AI Consultant and a member of its senior management team since 2022, responsible for technology, vendors, security, data and AI; wrote and owns its three-year IT plan (2023) and the three-year AI adoption plan (2025) that replaced it. Eleven years in technology, more than nine of them with international organisations. Bachelor of Science in Computer and Communication Engineering.

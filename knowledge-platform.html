04 · Retrieval platform, in pilot use

Retrieval-augmented knowledge platform with a citation on every answer

COAR Global, 2026. In pilot use with analysts.

Two search paths fused and reranked over a corpus that only an analyst's approval can extend, a citation on every claim, and an evaluation harness that makes retrieval quality a number. A pilot compared a generated assessment draft with the analysts' own, section by section, and showed where the model's value ends and the analyst's begins.

At a glance

Users
Analysts who draft assessments from district summaries, interviews and prior reporting. Senior analysts review each generated draft.
Problem
A large back-catalogue to dig through before writing. General-purpose chat tools cannot cite the organisation's own sources and should not hold its sensitive material.
Hypothesis
Retrieval with citations over an approved corpus removes the digging and gives a cited first draft, without putting the analyst's judgement in the model's hands.
What shipped
Hybrid retrieval with reranked, scored citations; approval-gated intake from the transcription pipelines; section-by-section drafting; an evaluation harness; a daily trend brief grounded in the corpus.
Decision owner
An analyst approves every item before it is indexed. Senior analysts judge each version in the evaluation loop.
Success measure
Hit rate, mean reciprocal rank, top relevance, latency and model-judged faithfulness from the harness, and the reviewers' verdict on whether a draft saves analyst time.

Four costs in an analyst's day

COAR Global is an international research organisation of about 180 staff that carries out research for United Nations agencies, with teams in Syria, Haiti, Myanmar and the Middle East and North Africa.

The two searches run in parallel. Every score shown to a user is the reranker's relevance, normalised 0 to 1, higher is better. An earlier pipeline showed raw cosine distance, which confused readers, and was replaced.

FastAPI serves the backend and Next.js the front end, behind nginx. A legacy pgvector pipeline with hosted embeddings remains as a switchable fallback.

Nothing enters the corpus without an analyst's sign-off: a submission becomes searchable only once it is approved in the admin view. The field submission's identifier is the de-duplication key, so a rerun of a pipeline is safe.

Measured

Retrieval quality is a measured number, not an impression. Any change to the pipeline is measured before and after.

The pilot

The pilot case was a regional joint assessment in Myanmar (2026). Analysts compiled the reference files: the finalised assessment drafts and the district summaries of community interviews. The workflow to reproduce: verify, summarise and analyse the district summaries to inform the final draft.

The analysts' draft also drew on interviews and secondary sources outside the eight documents.

  • Where both exist, they agree. No factual contradiction, and quotes are reproduced verbatim.
  • Seven empty sections each had a cited first draft. That is the time saving to test.
  • Out of reach: history, actor profiles, population and displacement totals, and anything from interviews or secondary sources that were not indexed. The strongest passages of the analysts' draft come from there.
  • Retrieval gaps are the main quality risk, not hallucination. In three places the draft said the sources were silent while the fact sat in a document that section had not pulled. Each section's context was capped at 12 chunks.
  • Cross-source checking works. The draft repeatedly notes where the district summaries and the partner interviews differ instead of merging them.

Two versions, one lesson

Reviewer feedback becomes explicit rules, measured on each new version. The second round ran on the same data set, so the two rounds compare.

Conclusion recorded: precision and usefulness pull against each other when the model writes the narrative, and tightening the rules further will not fix it. The platform's value is therefore placed in retrieval, citations and first drafts; the analyst keeps the narrative.

Quality risks found

What is next

  • Proposed: a scored evaluation. Run the same pipeline on the previous year's assessment set and score the generated draft against the published report with the harness (hit rate, MRR, faithfulness), to get a number rather than a reading.
  • Proposed: a larger context budget per section and a second pass per theme, measured before and after.
  • Decided: pilot use continues until the evaluation loop says otherwise. A short plain-language note on how the workflow, rules and evidence work was agreed for the research group.

What was learned

  • The failures were retrieval failures. With a citation on every claim, what went wrong was a fact that a section had not pulled. The proposed fix sits in retrieval.
  • The model's value ends where the narrative begins. Stricter rules made the draft more precise and no more useful. Retrieval, citations and first drafts are the platform's part; context and judgement are the analyst's.
  • Text on the page is rarely overruled. So every claim has to tie directly to its input, and the method has to be shown.

Role
